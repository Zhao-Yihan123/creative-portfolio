My sociological training has shaped a global perspective and a curiosity about how people think, behave, and interact. I use quantitative and qualitative research methods, including data cleaning and analysis in R, to understand complex social issues and audience needs.

Beyond academics, I have hands-on experience in cross-border e-commerce, content creation, event planning, and project coordination. I enjoy connecting research insights with practical execution—using digital tools, creative strategies, and teamwork to turn ideas into meaningful outcomes.
